Privacy Policy

Countdowns · Last updated 17 October 2026

Countdowns does not collect, store or share any personal data. The developer never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

Your calendar and reminders

With your permission, Countdowns reads your calendar and reminders on your device, only to show countdowns to the items you choose. Nothing from your calendar or reminders is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

iCloud sync

The countdowns you create are stored on your device. They sync between your own devices through your iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit private database. The developer can't access that data. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings.

No tracking

Countdowns has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
