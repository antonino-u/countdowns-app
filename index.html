Countdowns Support

For iPhone, iPad and Mac

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email urbcor@urbano.me. Please include your device, its OS version, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

Why does Countdowns need access to my calendar?

Each countdown is linked to an event on your calendar, so the calendar stays the source of truth: move the event and the countdown follows it. If you said no at first, turn access on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars (on a Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars).

A countdown says "Link Broken"

The event it was linked to was deleted from your calendar, or can no longer be found. Delete the countdown and add a new one linked to the right event.

My countdowns don't appear on my other devices

Countdowns syncs through iCloud. Check that each device is signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that each has a network connection. Syncing can take a few minutes.

Can I count down to a repeating event?

A countdown follows the single occurrence you picked, not the whole series.

The widget looks out of date

The system limits how often widgets can refresh. Opening the app usually updates it. If it stays stuck, remove the widget and add it again.
